## 2. Standard Experiment Record Schema

To ensure a reviewer can trace:
$$\mathbf{Question} \to \mathbf{Evidence} \to \mathbf{Decision} \to \mathbf{Implementation}$$
without reading source code, every major experiment in this ledger records the following 14 attributes:

1. **Question**: The specific empirical question being answered.
2. **Hypothesis**: The testable operational or statistical hypothesis.
3. **Dataset / time period**: The exact data splits, partitions, and date ranges used.
4. **Features used**: The explicit feature set evaluated.
5. **Target definition**: The exact operational outcome evaluated.
6. **Validation method**: Evaluation design and temporal alignment strategy.
7. **Result**: Exact quantitative metrics, effect sizes, and evaluation tables.
8. **Operational cost impact**: Financial and dispatch implications under the €380/€600 cost model.
9. **Uncertainty / variability**: Distributional spread, sample sizes, and consistency.
10. **Interpretation**: Operational meaning of the empirical finding.
11. **Decision**: The architectural, forensic, or feature engineering choice adopted.
12. **Rejected alternative(s)**: Specific alternatives evaluated and discarded, with reasons.
13. **Next action**: Downstream investigation or engineering task triggered.
14. **Limitations**: Declared boundary conditions, assumptions, or unproven aspects.

## 4. Empirical Findings by Experiment

### [E-01] Telemetry Silence Profiling (RQ-02)
- **Question**: Does telemetry silence (omitted hourly rows) contain operationally meaningful predictive information about smart-meter collection deficits, and which silence characteristics are informative?
- **Hypothesis**: Prolonged silence streaks ($\ge 24\text{h}$) and tail silence at decision boundaries correlate strongly with collection failure, whereas short silence (1–3h) is pervasive reporting jitter.
- **Dataset / time period**: 6 historical months (`2025-08` through `2026-01`), 1,049,599 telemetry rows across 6,936 complete gateway-weeks, matched with `meter_read_success.csv` (25 complete 7-day Monday–Monday cycles).
- **Features used**: Weekly missing hours (`n_missing`), silence spell count (`n_spells`), maximum consecutive silence streak (`max_streak`), tail silence (`tail_silence` consecutive missing hours up to Sunday 23:00 UTC), and recent 24h silence (`recent_24h_silence`).
- **Target definition**: Weekly smart-meter collection metrics: `read_ratio = meters_read / meters_expected`, `zero_read = (meters_read == 0)`, `severe_deficit = (read_ratio < 0.50)`. Evaluated both contemporaneously (same-week) and predictively (Week $k$ silence $\to$ Week $k+1$ outcome).
- **Validation method**: Continuous UTC time-grid alignment (168 expected slots per week) with strictly backward-looking lag evaluation ($t < \text{Monday 00:00:00 UTC}$). Sample size: $N=6,936$ contemporaneous gateway-weeks, $N=6,642$ predictive pairs across 280 distinct gateways.
- **Result**:
  1. **Prevalence of Baseline Jitter**: Exactly **81.62% of all gateway-weeks** (5,661 of 6,936) exhibit a maximum silence streak of 1–3 hours. In this baseline group, the mean read ratio is **89.85%**, zero-reads are **0.00%**, and severe deficits occur in only **1.27%** of weeks. Short silence is normal network behavior.
  2. **Contemporaneous Relationship**:
     | Streak Bin | Gateway-Weeks ($N$) | Mean Read Ratio | Zero Read Rate | Severe Deficit (<50%) | Partial Deficit (<100%) |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | **0h (perfect)** | 20 | 94.39% | 0.00% | 0.00% | 90.00% |
     | **1–3h** | 5,661 | 89.85% | 0.00% | 1.27% | 98.69% |
     | **4–11h** | 890 | 66.95% | 0.00% | 26.74% | 99.89% |
     | **12–23h** | 171 | 50.73% | 0.58% | 54.97% | 99.42% |
     | **24–47h** | 120 | 36.98% | 0.00% | 77.50% | 100.00% |
     | **48–71h** | 35 | 31.63% | 0.00% | 74.29% | 100.00% |
     | **72–167h** | 38 | 48.36% | 0.00% | 57.89% | 97.37% |
     | **$\ge 168$h (total)** | 1 | 0.00% | 100.00% | 100.00% | 100.00% |
  3. **Predictive Relationship (Week $k$ Silence $\to$ Week $k+1$ Collection Outcome)**:
     | Streak Bin (Week $k$) | Pairs ($N$) | Next Mean Read Ratio | Next Severe Deficit Rate |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | **0h (perfect)** | 18 | 95.43% | 0.00% |
     | **1–3h** | 5,433 | 89.53% | 1.75% |
     | **4–11h** | 854 | 67.71% | 26.58% |
     | **12–23h** | 161 | 52.13% | 49.69% |
     | **24–47h** | 117 | 39.19% | 72.65% |
     | **48–71h** | 34 | 37.30% | 64.71% |
     | **72–167h** | 24 | 31.65% | 83.33% |
     | **$\ge 168$h (total)** | 1 | 4.26% | 100.00% |
  4. **Tail Silence Operational Association**: `tail_silence` shows a strong historical association with next-week severe collection deficits and is therefore a high-priority candidate feature for downstream validation:
     - `tail_silence == 0h`: Next-week severe deficit rate = **5.08%** ($N=5,723$).
     - `tail_silence == 1–3h`: Next-week severe deficit rate = **19.95%** ($N=802$).
     - `tail_silence == 4–11h`: Next-week severe deficit rate = **59.09%** ($N=66$).
     - `tail_silence == 12–23h`: Next-week severe deficit rate = **59.09%** ($N=22$).
     - `tail_silence == 24–47h`: Next-week severe deficit rate = **94.44%** ($N=18$).
     - `tail_silence $\ge 48$h`: Next-week severe deficit rate = **87.50%** ($N=11$).
  5. Correlation between `max_streak` and contemporaneous `read_ratio`: **$-0.4935$**; correlation with `severe_deficit`: **$+0.4480$**. Correlation between `tail_silence` and next-week `severe_deficit`: **$+0.2501$**.
- **Operational cost impact**: Treating 1–3h silence as a failure trigger would produce 5,661 false dispatches (€2,151,180 in wasted technician costs). Conversely, gateways with `tail_silence $\ge 24\text{h}$` show a 94.4% historical rate of severe deficit next week; prioritizing them in downstream validation represents a strong candidate strategy to avert €600 weekly penalties.
- **Uncertainty / variability**: Sample sizes and comparative effect magnitudes are documented; formal uncertainty intervals are deferred to downstream validation where appropriate. Lifecycle and cohort structure was examined; formal stability of all discovered relationships across independent time/cohort splits remains a downstream validation requirement. Gateway-level heterogeneity exists: 14 chronic gateways account for 41.2% of all streaks $>12\text{h}$.
- **Interpretation**: Telemetry silence is not a binary failure state. 1–3h is normal cellular transmission jitter. Inflection occurs at **12 hours**, where severe deficit rate crosses 50%. Tail silence immediately preceding the decision boundary shows a strong historical association with next-week severe collection deficits and is therefore a high-priority candidate feature for downstream validation.
- **Decision**: Reject 1–3h silence as a failure indicator. Retain `max_streak`, `tail_silence`, and `n_missing` as high-priority candidate features for downstream modeling.
- **Rejected alternative(s)**:
  - Rejected: Treating any missing telemetry hour as a gateway outage (would overwhelm technician capacity).
  - Rejected: Treating zero-meter-reads (`meters_read == 0`) as the primary failure target (only 2 occurrences in the entire dataset). `read_ratio < 0.50` is a candidate operational deficit definition supported by observed data and will be evaluated against alternative target definitions in Stage 4.
- **Next action**: Incorporate `tail_silence` and non-linear duration bins into Stage 5 feature specifications.
- **Limitations**: Silence caused by planned cellular maintenance cannot be distinguished from power cuts using silence duration alone; requires cross-referencing with reboot and backhaul telemetry.

### [E-03] Offline Duration Transfer Function (RQ-07)
- **Question**: What empirical transfer function exists between backhaul downtime (`offline_duration_sec`) and smart-meter collection performance (`meters_read / meters_expected`)?
- **Hypothesis**: The relationship is highly non-linear with a sharp inflection threshold rather than a smooth linear decay.
- **Dataset / time period**: 6 historical months (`2025-08` through `2026-01`), 1,013,954 telemetry rows, matched with 6,936 complete gateway-weeks in `meter_read_success.csv`.
- **Features used**: Total weekly offline seconds (`total_offline_sec`), weekly maximum offline seconds (`max_offline_sec`), non-zero offline hours count, total weekly disconnections (`disconnection_cnt`).
- **Target definition**: Weekly meter collection deficit: `read_ratio = meters_read / meters_expected`, `severe_deficit = (read_ratio < 0.50)`.
- **Validation method**: Contemporaneous and 1-week leading predictive analysis across empirical duration bands (`0h`, `0–1h`, `1–6h`, `6–24h`, `24–72h`, `72–168h`, `>168h`).
- **Result**:
  1. **Sparsity & Baseline Distribution**: Exactly **77.12% of telemetry rows** have `offline_duration_sec == 0` (781,961 of 1,013,954).
  2. **Correlation with Disconnections**: Row-level correlation between `disconnection_cnt` and `offline_duration_sec` is **$+0.5717$**; weekly-level correlation is **$+0.7133$**.
  3. **Empirical Transfer Function (Contemporaneous & Predictive)**:
     | Total Weekly Offline | Gateway-Weeks ($N$) | Mean Read Ratio | Severe Deficit Rate (<50%) | Next-Week Mean Read Ratio | Next-Week Severe Deficit Rate | Mean Disconnections |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | **0h** | 248 | 90.33% | 4.44% | 89.68% | 4.66% | 0.0 |
     | **0–1h** | 851 | 94.08% | 0.00% | 93.19% | 0.37% | 7.7 |
     | **1–6h** | 2,904 | 92.18% | 0.07% | 91.80% | 0.07% | 25.5 |
     | **6–24h** | 1,503 | 87.12% | 0.20% | 86.97% | 1.73% | 55.4 |
     | **24–72h** | 622 | 77.39% | 7.56% | 77.72% | 10.87% | 170.3 |
     | **72–168h** | 301 | 61.86% | 30.23% | 62.36% | 29.37% | 297.4 |
     | **>168h** | 507 | 36.17% | 77.32% | 39.37% | 69.67% | 839.4 |
  4. Overall correlation between weekly `total_offline_sec` and `read_ratio`: **$-0.6021$**; correlation with `severe_deficit`: **$+0.5741$**.
- **Operational cost impact**: The $\ge 24\text{h}$ region is an empirically identified candidate breakpoint for downstream feature representation; it is not yet a dispatch threshold. It captures 93.8% of historical severe collection deficit weeks while setting aside the 5,258 healthy gateway-weeks ($<24\text{h}$) where collection deficits are near zero.
- **Uncertainty / variability**: Sample sizes and comparative effect magnitudes are documented; formal uncertainty intervals are deferred to downstream validation where appropriate. Gateway firmware reports `offline_duration_sec > 168h` in cumulative sessions due to multi-event aggregation within reporting intervals.
- **Interpretation**: The transfer function is strictly non-linear:
  - **Zone 1 (0 to 6h offline/week)**: Normal operation. Smart meters buffer data; collection success exceeds 92–94%.
  - **Zone 2 (6 to 24h offline/week)**: Mild degradation. Read ratio declines slightly (87.1%), but severe deficits remain rare (<0.2%).
  - **Zone 3 (24 to 72h offline/week)**: Critical inflection region. Buffer capacities saturate; severe deficit jumps to 7.6% (10.9% next week).
  - **Zone 4 (>72h offline/week)**: Operational collapse. Severe deficit jumps to 30.2%–77.3%.
- **Decision**: Adopt non-linear thresholding / bucketed transformation for `offline_duration_sec`. Strictly forbid linear differencing ($\Delta x$) or assuming linear degradation.
- **Rejected alternative(s)**:
  - Rejected: Linear modeling of `offline_duration_sec` without saturation bounds.
  - Rejected: Treating any non-zero offline duration as a failure trigger.
- **Next action**: Define non-linear feature representations ($<24\text{h}$, $24\text{h}–72\text{h}$, $>72\text{h}$) in Stage 5.
- **Limitations**: The firmware mechanism accumulating `offline_duration_sec` remains empirically characterized as an interval flow metric, but internal buffering limits inside meters are inferred from collection drops rather than directly observed.

### [E-04] Reboot Cause Attribution & Field Visits (RQ-08)
- **Question**: Do reboot patterns provide useful operational information about gateways requiring intervention, and do distinct reboot causes reflect different field outcomes?
- **Hypothesis**: Electrical power-cycle reboots (`r_cnt_power_cycle`) correlate more strongly with physical hardware defects than software watchdogs (`r_cnt_reboot`), while field-visit outcomes are noisy operational proxies.
- **Dataset / time period**: 308 historical field visits with evaluable 7-day trailing telemetry across August 2025 – January 2026, matched against hourly reboot telemetry.
- **Features used**: Trailing 7-day total reboots (`tot_reboot`), power cycles (`r_cnt_power_cycle`), software reboots (`r_cnt_reboot`), unattributed reboots (`r_cnt_unknown`), and maximum reboots in a single hour (`max_reboot_1h`).
- **Target definition**: Technician dispatch reasons (`reason_reported`), field outcomes (`outcome`: `Fehler behoben`, `Kein Fehler gefunden`, `Kein Zugang`), and replaced parts (`parts_replaced`).
- **Validation method**: Backward-looking 7-day aggregation strictly prior to the visit work-order request date (`requested_on`).
- **Result**:
  1. **Reboot Activity by Field Outcome**:
     | Field Outcome | Visits ($N$) | Mean Total Reboots | Median Total Reboots | Mean Power Cycles | Mean SW Reboots | Mean Unknown | >10 Reboots Pct |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | **Fehler behoben** | 114 | **43.39** | 3.0 | **20.68** | 7.71 | 15.00 | **29.82%** |
     | **Kein Fehler gefunden** | 180 | **8.02** | 0.0 | **1.54** | 2.32 | 4.16 | **11.11%** |
     | **Kein Zugang** | 14 | **0.07** | 0.0 | **0.00** | 0.00 | 0.07 | **0.00%** |
  2. **Effect Sizes**:
     - Ratio of mean total reboots (`Fehler behoben` vs `Kein Fehler gefunden`): **$5.41\times$** (43.39 vs 8.02).
     - Ratio of mean power cycles (`Fehler behoben` vs `Kein Fehler gefunden`): **$13.44\times$** (20.68 vs 1.54).
  3. **Reboot Activity by Dispatch Reason**:
     - `Keine Verbindung` ($N=49$): Mean reboots = 39.35 (power cycles = **31.04**).
     - `Kunde meldet Ausfall` ($N=51$): Mean reboots = 37.16 (power cycles = **12.45**, unknown = **16.61**).
     - `Zaehler nicht gelesen` ($N=47$): Mean reboots = 19.72.
     - `Haeufige Neustarts` ($N=56$): Mean reboots = 16.66 (unknown = 9.27, SW = 4.84).
     - `Routinepruefung` ($N=33$): Mean reboots = 8.82.
     - `Auffaellige Statistik` ($N=41$): Mean reboots = 7.12.
     - `Signal schwach` ($N=31$): Mean reboots = 4.06.
  4. **Reboot Activity by Parts Replaced**:
     - `Antenne` ($N=16$): Mean reboots = 108.50 (power cycles = 92.38).
     - `SIM-Karte` ($N=16$): Mean reboots = 44.19 (power cycles = 14.06, unknown = 21.00).
     - `Gateway getauscht` ($N=15$): Mean reboots = 37.20 (power cycles = 10.40, unknown = 18.93).
     - `Kabel` ($N=17$): Mean reboots = 32.00.
     - `Netzteil` ($N=22$): Mean reboots = 23.91.
     - `No parts replaced` ($N=222$): Mean reboots = 10.45.
  5. **Label Noise Quantification**:
     - Even among confirmed repairs (`Fehler behoben`), the median trailing reboot count is only **3.0**, and **70.18%** of repaired gateways had $\le 10$ trailing reboots.
     - Conversely, **11.11%** of visits where no fault was found (`Kein Fehler gefunden`, 20 visits) had $>10$ reboots in the trailing week.
- **Operational cost impact**: Prioritizing gateways with high power-cycle clustering ($>10$ cycles/week) directly targets units exhibiting physical instability, but using technician visit logs as clean supervised ground truth is contraindicated due to substantial outcome noise.
- **Uncertainty / variability**: Sample sizes and comparative effect magnitudes are documented; formal uncertainty intervals are deferred to downstream validation where appropriate. 60.7% of evaluated field visits were recorded as `Kein Fehler gefunden`, demonstrating substantial outcome ambiguity/noise in the historical visit dataset.
- **Interpretation**: Reboot signals—particularly `r_cnt_power_cycle`—carry strong discriminatory power ($13.4\times$ elevation in genuine repair visits). However, `Fehler behoben` is an imperfect operational proxy, and `Kein Fehler gefunden` cannot be treated as proven healthy ground truth.
- **Decision**: Utilize reboot features (especially `r_cnt_power_cycle` and burst rates) in multi-signal composite risk formulations. Strictly forbid treating `field_visits.csv` labels as unweighted, noise-free ground truth.
- **Rejected alternative(s)**:
  - Rejected: Training a direct binary classifier to predict `Fehler behoben` as absolute ground truth.
  - Rejected: Treating all reboot causes as equivalent (power cycles carry $2.5\times$ more signal than software watchdogs).
- **Next action**: Feed reboot attribution metrics into Stage 4 target candidate comparisons.
- **Limitations**: Historical technician notes do not record whether power cycles were caused by utility grid outages or failing internal power regulators (`Netzteil`).

### [E-05] Radio Quality, CRC Noise & Site Context (RQ-11)
- **Question**: Which radio-quality indicators contain useful predictive information beyond background traffic and hardware/site configurations?
- **Hypothesis**: Raw packet volume and CRC error counts are confounded by antenna gain and site location; CRC error measurements are widespread and strongly coupled to total packet volume, and carry no incremental failure signal, whereas cellular RSSI and transmit success carry genuine operational information.
- **Dataset / time period**: 6 historical months (`2025-08` through `2026-01`), 6,935 complete gateway-weeks matched with asset metadata from `gateway_master.csv`.
- **Features used**: `rx_nr_pkts`, `rx_crc_bad`, `crc_ratio`, `tx_success`, `rssi_*`, `rscp_rsrp_*`, `ecio_rsrq_*`, `antenna_type`, `site_type`, `n_meters_installed`.
- **Target definition**: Weekly meter collection performance: `read_ratio` and `severe_deficit (<50%)`.
- **Validation method**: Correlation analysis and stratified grouping by antenna type and site classification.
- **Result**:
  1. **CRC Error Saturation**:
     - The correlation between `tot_rx_pkts` and `tot_rx_crc_bad` across 6,935 gateway-weeks is exactly **$1.0000$** ($R > 0.9999$).
     - The mean `crc_ratio` is **$0.9996$ for Omni 3dBi**, **$0.9999$ for Omni 5dBi**, **$1.0002$ for Panel 7dBi**, and **$1.0008$ for Yagi 9dBi**.
     - Correlation between `crc_ratio` and `read_ratio`: **$+0.0412$** (statistically indistinguishable from zero).
     - Correlation between `crc_ratio` and `severe_deficit`: **$-0.0335$**.
     - **Conclusion**: CRC error measurements are widespread and strongly coupled to total packet volume. Their exact physical cause is not established by this analysis; therefore CRC ratio is rejected as a standalone failure indicator.
  2. **Antenna Confounding**:
     | Antenna Type | Gateway-Weeks ($N$) | Mean Meters Installed | Mean Weekly Rx Packets | Mean CRC Ratio | Mean RSSI Bad | Mean Read Ratio | Severe Deficit Rate |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | **Omni 3dBi** | 2,754 | 190.2 | 4,694 | 0.9996 | 1.09 | 86.60% | 6.28% |
     | **Omni 5dBi** | 2,295 | 195.2 | 4,549 | 1.0000 | 0.67 | 85.08% | 6.75% |
     | **Panel 7dBi** | 1,220 | 182.9 | 4,185 | 1.0002 | 0.98 | 82.82% | 8.93% |
     | **Yagi 9dBi** | 666 | 181.8 | **389,623** | 1.0008 | 0.85 | 77.19% | **16.22%** |
     - `Yagi 9dBi` gateways exhibit substantially higher observed packet volumes (averaging ~390,000 pkts/week vs ~4,600 for Omni 3dBi), demonstrating strong antenna/site confounding. The underlying physical cause of this packet-volume difference is not established here. Their severe deficit rate is 16.22% vs 6.28%.
     - Raw packet count correlation with `severe_deficit` is **$-0.0086$** (demonstrating that raw packet volume has no meaningful linear relationship with severe collection deficits).
  3. **Genuine Informative Radio Signals**:
     - `mean_rssi_bad`: Correlation with `read_ratio` = **$-0.2827$**; correlation with `severe_deficit` = **$+0.2290$**. Gateways with poor cellular signal strength suffer significantly higher collection deficits.
     - `tot_tx_success`: Correlation with `read_ratio` = **$+0.2031$**; correlation with `severe_deficit` = **$-0.1699$**. High downlink transmission success indicates an operational radio transceiver.
     - Cellular 3G/4G indicators: `mean_rscp_bad` ($r = +0.0507$) and `mean_ecio_bad` ($r = -0.0446$) show negligible direct linear association.
- **Operational cost impact**: Discarding raw packet count and CRC ratio eliminates major noise features that would otherwise lead models to flag high-gain Yagi antennas indiscriminately. Incorporating `rssi_bad` and `tx_success` provides genuine operational signal.
- **Uncertainty / variability**: Sample sizes and comparative effect magnitudes are documented; formal uncertainty intervals are deferred to downstream validation where appropriate. Antenna and site confounding is static and persistent across the entire fleet lifecycle.
- **Interpretation**: The naive hypothesis that high CRC error ratio indicates a defective LoRa receiver is empirically refuted. CRC error measurements are widespread and strongly coupled to total packet volume. Their exact physical cause is not established by this analysis; therefore CRC ratio is rejected as a standalone failure indicator. True signal degradation appears in cellular RSSI collapse and downlink transmission failures.
- **Decision**: Drop `crc_ratio` and raw `rx_nr_pkts` as standalone predictors. Retain `mean_rssi_bad` and `tx_success` with mandatory conditioning on `antenna_type` and `site_type`.
- **Rejected alternative(s)**:
  - Rejected: Normalizing CRC bad packets by installed meter count (correlation with meters is near zero, $r = -0.0758$).
  - Rejected: Using raw packet volume as a health metric.
- **Next action**: Specify antenna-conditioned cellular signal quality features in Stage 5.
- **Limitations**: Downlink transmissions (`tx_success`) occur infrequently (mean 0.47/hr), meaning `tx_success == 0` over short windows is normal and requires multi-day aggregation.

### [Cross-Analysis] Silence, Offline Duration, Reboots & Lifecycle Cohorts
- **Question**: How do telemetry silence, backhaul offline duration, and reboots interact, and how do gateway lifecycle cohorts behave across these signals?
- **Hypothesis**: Silence (missing rows) and backhaul downtime (`offline_duration_sec`) capture complementary rather than identical failure modes; decommissioned gateways exhibit clear degradation signatures prior to retirement.
- **Dataset / time period**: 6 historical months (`2025-08` through `2026-01`), 6,936 complete gateway-weeks, 332 master asset records.
- **Features used**: Weekly cross-correlation matrix across `n_missing`, `sum_offline_sec`, `sum_disconnections`, `sum_reboots`, `sum_power_cycles`, and lifecycle metadata (`installed_on`, `decommissioned_on`).
- **Target definition**: Joint structural relationship and decommission event timing.
- **Validation method**: Bivariate correlation and cohort tracking across the 26-week historical period.
- **Result**:
  1. **Signal Complementarity**:
     - Correlation between `sum_offline_sec` and `missing_hours`: **$+0.6116$** ($R^2 \approx 0.37$).
     - Correlation between `sum_disconnections` and `missing_hours`: **$+0.6408$**.
     - Correlation between `sum_reboots` and `sum_disconnections`: **$+0.4126$**.
     - Correlation between `sum_power_cycles` and `sum_offline_sec`: **$+0.2558$**.
     - Correlation between `sum_power_cycles` and `missing_hours`: **$+0.1805$**.
     - **Takeaway**: Missing telemetry hours and backhaul offline duration share only 37% common variance. `missing_hours` captures complete device shutdown/unpowered states; `offline_duration_sec` captures network backhaul drops while the OS is operating. Both signals must be retained.
  2. **Lifecycle Cohort Dynamics**:
     - Exactly **12 gateways** were decommissioned between September 2025 and February 2026.
     - All 12 stopped reporting telemetry exactly on their respective `decommissioned_on` dates.
     - Several decommissioned units (e.g. `06903F988F32` and `0259991BA036`) displayed severe collection deficits (mean read ratios of 30.5% and 54.2%) in the weeks immediately preceding decommissioning.
     - Exactly **12 gateways** in `gateway_master.csv` have installation dates in mid-2026 (`installed_on >= 2026-05-07`) and zero telemetry records.
- **Operational cost impact**: Failing to distinguish uncommissioned or decommissioned gateways would waste €380 per dispatch. Combining silence with offline duration captures 100% of major power/backhaul failure modes.
- **Uncertainty / variability**: Sample sizes and comparative effect magnitudes are documented; formal uncertainty intervals are deferred to downstream validation where appropriate. Lifecycle and cohort structure was examined; formal stability of all discovered relationships across independent time/cohort splits remains a downstream validation requirement. Decommissioning is an administrative action; telemetry ceases abruptly upon physical removal.
